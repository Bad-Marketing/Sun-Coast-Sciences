Sun Coast Sciences Amazon Growth Strategy

Prepared by FTR  ·  Amazon growth for established DTC & CPG brands

Summary

Sun Coast Sciences has a strong Amazon business, supported by established hero products, growing sales, and substantial brand awareness generated through off-Amazon marketing.

Amazon revenue reached approximately $794K in October 2026, up from $372K in January. Total Package Serum (TPS) and RejuvaLush account for nearly 90% of revenue, making them the primary drivers of the business.

Our strategy for the next 90 days is focused on capturing more of the demand the brand already generates, improving the shopping experience, and creating more opportunities for customers to discover and purchase complementary products.

Key Priorities
  1. Strengthen branded search visibility and protection. Improve how effectively Sun Coast Sciences captures shoppers searching for the brand and its hero products, while reducing opportunities for competitors and lookalike products to intercept that demand.
  2. Optimize the two hero listings. Improve search-result appeal, product messaging, imagery, and supporting content for TPS and RejuvaLush.
  3. Build a stronger customer journey. Rework the Brand Store and Brand Story to help shoppers discover the wider product range and build complementary routines.
  4. Introduce bundles and strengthen cross-selling. Use the hero products to introduce customers to additional skincare and haircare products.
  5. Improve retention and repeat purchases. Activate Brand Tailored Promotions and refine Subscribe & Save offers.
  6. Test incremental customer acquisition through Creator Connections. Reach relevant new audiences with focused, routine-based creator campaigns without significantly expanding non-branded PPC investment.
Growth Area 1 | Branded Search Capture & Protection
Opportunity

Sun Coast Sciences generates substantial branded demand through its off-Amazon marketing efforts.

Amazon Search Query Performance data shows strong purchase share across branded searches. However, competitors are actively advertising against the brand's product names, creating opportunities to intercept shoppers who are already looking for Sun Coast Sciences.

In October:

  • Sun Coast Sciences brand-name searches captured 77% of clicks and 87% of purchases.
  • TPS product-name searches captured 71.4% of clicks and 81.7% of purchases.
  • RejuvaLush product-name searches captured 71.3% of clicks and 82.8% of purchases.
  • For the exact search term "total package serum," approximately 20.5% of purchases went to products outside the brand.

The opportunity is to strengthen branded visibility, improve click share, and protect the demand the brand is already paying to generate.

Key Recommendations
  • Strengthen branded PPC coverage for Sun Coast Sciences, Total Package Serum, and RejuvaLush, including relevant product-name variations and misspellings.
  • Review Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Sponsored Brands Video opportunities to improve visibility across branded search results.
  • Improve hero-product main images to strengthen differentiation and encourage more clicks from search results.
  • Review competing lookalike listings and address intellectual property violations through Amazon Brand Registry where applicable.
  • Monitor branded click share, purchase share, and total sales to evaluate the impact of additional advertising without unnecessarily increasing spend on orders that would otherwise occur organically.
Growth Area 2 | Hero Listing Optimization
Opportunity

TPS and RejuvaLush account for nearly 90% of Amazon revenue, making their listings the highest-priority conversion opportunities.

Both products have strong demand, but their current listing content leaves room for improvement.

Total Package Serum

TPS has an established sales history and strong organic visibility. However, its imagery and A+ Content could do more to communicate the product's benefits, differentiate it from competitors, and reinforce its simple daily skincare positioning.

The current content also offers limited opportunities to introduce complementary products.

RejuvaLush

RejuvaLush has experienced strong recent growth, but its listing requires more extensive optimization.

The title, product highlights and image stack need refinement, and the listing currently has no A+ Content or Brand Story.

Key Recommendations
  • Complete full listing optimizations for TPS and RejuvaLush, including copy, image stack, Premium A+ upgrade and backend.
  • Strengthen hero-product positioning:
    • TPS: Four serums in one, simplified daily skincare, and benefits relevant to mature skin.
    • RejuvaLush: Haircare benefits, consistent use, and realistic expectations around results.
  • Incorporate routine-building, product usage, and supported benefit-timeline messaging to help customers understand how to get the most from each product.
  • Review customer feedback and address recurring complaints, and product-experience concerns through listing content where appropriate.
  • Introduce complementary products within A+ Content to encourage discovery beyond the initial purchase.
Growth Area 3 | Brand Store & Customer Journey Optimization
Opportunity

The Brand Store already attracts meaningful traffic, generating approximately 4K visitors and $31K in attributed sales over the last 30 days.

However, the Store currently has a Medium quality rating with limited product education, routine-based shopping, and opportunities to guide customers from hero products into the wider catalog.

With approximately 71% of visitors new to the Store, there is a clear opportunity to improve how the brand introduces the full product range.

Key Recommendations
  • Rebuild the Brand Store with clearer navigation, stronger product education, and improved mobile shopping experiences.
  • Create dedicated shopping journeys that help customers discover products based on their needs, interests, and complementary routines.
  • Introduce routine-based sections that connect hero products with relevant complementary products.
  • Rebuild Brand Story content with clickable product and Store navigation to support cross-selling directly from listings.
  • Use the Store to feature bundles, complementary products, and relevant promotions.
Growth Area 4 | Bundles & Cross-Sell Strategy
Opportunity

The Amazon business is heavily concentrated in TPS and RejuvaLush, while the wider product catalog contributes a relatively small share of revenue.

There are currently no bundles available, despite several opportunities to combine products into complementary selfcare routines.

Introducing bundles would help the brand leverage existing hero-product demand while making it easier for customers to discover and purchase additional products.

Key Recommendations
  • Introduce a focused selection of routine-based bundles, starting with combinations built around TPS and RejuvaLush.
  • Launch Virtual Bundles as the initial approach.
  • Feature bundles throughout the Brand Store and relevant cross-sell placements.
Growth Area 5 | Customer Retention & Subscribe & Save
Opportunity

Sun Coast Sciences already has an established Subscribe & Save program, with 4,448 active subscriptions.

Active subscriptions increased approximately 32% between August and early November, showing encouraging growth.

TPS has a substantial subscriber base, while RejuvaLush has relatively low enrollment and currently offers a 0% seller-funded S&S discount, compared with 10% on most other core products.

The brand also has substantial audiences available through Brand Tailored Promotions. These audiences are not currently being leveraged through active promotions.

Key Recommendations
  • Increase RejuvaLush's seller-funded S&S discount from 0% to 10%, aligning it with the broader product range.
  • Maintain existing S&S offers across other products and monitor enrollment and revenue trends before introducing additional incentives.
  • Activate Brand Tailored Promotions for Abandoned Cart, Promising Customer, and Top-Tier Customer audiences.
  • Support repeat purchases through stronger routine, usage, and benefit-expectation messaging across listings and Store content.
  • Monitor active subscriptions, S&S revenue, promotional performance, and repeat-purchase opportunities.
Growth Area 6 | New Customer Acquisition Through Creator Connections
Opportunity

Sun Coast Sciences already benefits from substantial off-Amazon demand generation, particularly through Meta advertising.

The initial opportunity is to test a targeted approach to reaching new audiences through Amazon Creator Connections.

Creator-led content can introduce shoppers to the hero products while reinforcing the broader skincare and haircare routines we are building throughout the Amazon shopping experience.

Key Recommendations
  • Review existing Creator Connections activity once access is available.
  • Launch two focused creator campaigns:
    • TPS + Complementary Skincare: Introduce TPS as the foundation of a simple skincare routine, supported by relevant complementary products.
    • RejuvaLush + Complementary Products: Introduce RejuvaLush as part of a consistent haircare routine, with relevant complementary products.
  • Prioritize creators with audiences aligned with the brand's core customer demographic.
  • Develop briefs focused on product education, authentic demonstrations, consistent use, and routine building.
Advertising Strategy
Opportunity

Sun Coast Sciences already has an efficient advertising account with a clear structure. Each product has its own portfolio. Recent work moving traffic from broad to phrase and exact match brought ACOS down from around 25% to 13%.

Almost all advertising goes to branded demand. This matches how the business grows: off-Amazon marketing creates the demand, and Amazon PPC captures it.

Over the last 60 days:

  • Sponsored Products generated approximately $617K in sales from $98K in spend (15.9% ACOS).
  • More than 95% of Sponsored Products spend went to branded and product-name searches.
  • On the most important branded search terms, Sun Coast Sciences ranks first among advertisers but holds only 15% to 26% of ad impressions. For example, it holds 25.7% on “total package serum” and 17.8% on “rejuvalush hair growth shampoo.”
  • There are currently no active Sponsored Brands or Sponsored Brands Video campaigns. This leaves the most visible placements on branded search results open to competitors.
  • Non-branded search terms account for less than 5% of spend and run at a much higher ACOS than branded terms.

The opportunity is to protect branded demand more completely, especially at the top of search results. At the same time, spend needs to stay disciplined so the brand isn’t paying for orders that would happen organically anyway.

Key Recommendations
Branded Search Capture & Protection
  • Keep the current product-based portfolio structure, and keep building on the broad, phrase and exact optimization approach that is already working.
  • Expand branded coverage to include product-name variations and common misspellings. October search data shows misspellings such as “rejuvelush shampoo” already driving meaningful sales.
  • Set branded bids using impression share and organic rank together. Where we already hold 90% to 100% of impressions, we will hold or lower bids to avoid paying for organic sales. Where our share is lower, we will increase visibility.
  • Monitor competitor activity on branded terms weekly and adjust bids quickly, since competitor spend on these terms changes from week to week.
Sponsored Brands & Sponsored Brands Video
  • Relaunch Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Brands Video on branded searches for Sun Coast Sciences, TPS and RejuvaLush to secure the top-of-search banner and video placements.
  • Use these placements to introduce complementary products and drive traffic to the rebuilt Brand Store, supporting the bundle and cross-sell strategy.
  • Align ad creative with the refreshed listing imagery and the messaging customers already recognize from Meta.
Sponsored Display
  • Review the current Sponsored Display reach campaign, which spent approximately $5.9K for $5.3K in sales over the last 60 days.
  • Refocus Sponsored Display on defending our own product pages and re-engaging shoppers who viewed but did not purchase, with a focus on complementary products.
Focused Non-Branded Growth
  • Continue the recently launched funnel campaigns, which have shown strong early results.
  • Run small, controlled tests on high-intent non-branded searches that match the core customer, such as anti-aging face serums for women and shampoos for thinning hair.
  • Scale only the terms that convert profitably. Creator Connections remains the main lever for reaching new customers.
Account Management
  • Confirm and turn off any remaining Helium 10 PPC automations, so that all bid and budget changes are managed in one place.
  • Exclude legacy ASINs from all campaigns and keep advertising focused on the main active listings.
  • Pace spend around TPS and RejuvaLush inventory levels and the recent TPS price change, to avoid driving traffic we can’t fulfill.
How We Will Measure Success
  • Total Amazon sales and TACOS, rather than ACOS alone.
  • Branded impression share, click share and purchase share for Sun Coast Sciences, TPS and RejuvaLush.
  • Share of purchases on “total package serum” going to the brand, currently around 79.5%.
  • ACOS and sales from non-branded tests against agreed targets.
Creative Strategy

Premium look with clarity in the content: large type and real proof (doctor, ingredients, timeline, age appropriate women), explained calmly.

Key points: existing imagery appears outdated and inconsistent. Models and images don’t fully convey ‘premium’. This makes product/brand trust difficult for NTB consumers.

We need to ensure:

  1. Imagery is aimed at our target audience through models and lifestyle images
  2. Branding is consistent and all imagery flows cleanly - elevate to justify premium price point
  3. Product USPs are highlighted early on, and education continues throughout the consumer buying journey across all elements
  4. Objections (negative reviews) are addressed clearly and calmly throughout
  5. Optimized for readability on mobile
  6. Clinical but with added warmth to encourage incorporation into daily routine
Main images
  • Recognition over decoration. "Total Package Serum," "RejuvaLush" and "Sun Coast Sciences" must read at mobile thumbnail size.
  • TPS: current packaging and 2026 formula. Bottle at a slight angle, box and a small swatch of the white cream to show the new texture honestly.
  • RejuvaLush: bottle front-on or at a slight angle, with an honest lather or texture detail if it helps the product read as a premium shampoo rather than a generic bottle.
  • Policy: no added text or badges. Multipacks show the exact count clearly.
  • Test via Manage Your Experiments, and track branded click share as the success metric.
USP hierarchy
  1. Is it legitimate? Doctor-designed by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, made in the USA in a cGMP facility in Florida, 2.4M bottles sold
  2. What does it do for me, at my age? Benefits for mature skin and hair, shown on age-appropriate women.
  3. Why this one? Key USPs highlighted within first 3 listing images.
  4. How does it work? Key actives, each mapped to a concern.
  5. When will I see it? Honest expectations of timelines and results.
  6. How do I use it? Simple daily use. Consistency is what produces results, which also sets up S&S.

All claims use cosmetic "appearance" language and carry "Results may vary," and all copy goes past Amazon restrictions check before final upload.

Objections (TPS): price ("about 30 days per bottle, four serums in one"; never %-off graphics), scent (name the citrus scent calmly), sensitive skin ("formulated for all skin types").

Bundles & cross-sell
  • Bundle creative: each bundle needs a clear "what's included" visual and a step-by-step "how to use your routine" image.
  • Placements: image 8 on both heroes; an A+ routine/comparison module; a Brand Story carousel with clickable navigation; Store routine sections; Sponsored Display on our own listings.
Subscribe & Save
  • Message: consistency is what produces results.
  • Offer-agnostic: "Save with Subscribe & Save," never a % figure.
  • Placements: the timeline image (both heroes), an A+ "Built for consistency" module, and a Store S&S section.
Store structure
  • Ensure navigation flows easily - highlight bestsellers, have pages for shop by routine.
  • Mobile first: large type, single-column tiles, clear labels. Aim to lift the Medium quality rating.
  • Highlight authenticity indicators to detract from resellers/counterfeits. e.g. "Only genuine when sold by Sun Coast Sciences."
Premium A+ Content
  • Use for further consumer education on products, as well as building brand trust and encouraging product discovery, cross selling and subscriptions.
  • Ensure branding flows neatly throughout and all imagery is appropriate for target consumer (older women and relevant lifestyle imagery).
Brand Story
  • Showcase bestsellers and range
  • Build brand trust by including information about Dr. Mark Rosenberg and brand mission
  • Link through to storefront, can be customized for events to lead to deals
